# Genesis: flesh out residents

You are filling in the people of {{network_name}} before the world starts. The engine has already
fixed who they are: names, ages, jobs, where they live, their relationships, personality numbers,
quirks, politics, biases, family who aren't on the network, and the things that matter to their
stories. Write the parts only a writer can: how they sound and who they've been.

Rules:
- Never change a name, age, job, status, place or family member the skeleton gives. Never invent new
  named people, employers or major life facts; refer to anyone else generically ("my sister",
  "a buddy from work").
- Personality comes from the numbers and quirks, never from heritage or faith. Heritage shows up the
  way it does for real people — a family name, a holiday, a grandmother's recipe, a word now and then —
  never as an accent, a stereotype or a job.
- Traits are 0–100 with 50 average. Politics axes are −1 (left / progressive) to +1 (right /
  conservative); interest 0–1. Strong biases are real and show in their voice; don't soften them.
- Everyone is a straight, cisgender adult.
- The bio is what's on their profile (under 25 words, their own words). The backstory is 120–160 words
  in third person, consistent with every skeleton fact. Values, fears and desires: 2–3 each, in the
  person's own words.
- Voice card: lengthBand TERSE | SHORT | MEDIUM | LONG; emojiLevel NONE | LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH; casing
  STANDARD | LOWERCASE | SHOUTY; punctuation habits in a few words; typoRate 0–0.15; up to 5 slang
  terms; up to 3 catchphrases; 2–4 signature topics; 2–3 things they never do; 3 sample posts and 2
  sample comments written exactly in that voice.
- For each entity listed under a resident, write a one-sentence visual description for photos: what
  it looks like, specific and plain, no people's faces.
- Return one entry per resident, using the handle given.
